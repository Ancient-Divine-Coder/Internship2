Every blade earns its edge

We start with raw billet, not pre-ground stock. Each piece is hand-hammered at forging temperature, folded where the steel calls for it, then quenched and tempered by eye.

  • Hand-forged from raw billet, never cast
  • Clay-tempered or oil-quenched depending on blade class
  • Full-tang construction on every model
  • Each blade signed and dated by the smith

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these blades sharp out of the box?

Battle Ready models ship with a working edge. Display and Collector's Edition pieces ship blunt by default — tell us at checkout if you'd like a live edge instead.

What steel do you use?

Mostly 1095 high-carbon and hand-folded Damascus, depending on the model. Full specs are listed on every product card.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, though blade imports are regulated differently in every country — check your local laws before ordering, we can't do that part for you.

What's the return policy?

30 days on unsharpened and unused blades. Custom-etched or personalized orders are final sale.
